# Europedia: shared EU5 checks

The checked-in GUI/API references do not expose a verified data registration point for an arbitrary new Europedia page. A custom tab may require an override of `in_game/gui/encyclopedia_lateralview.gui` with mutually exclusive content trees; inspect current vanilla structure and other loaded mods before choosing that route. Treat same-path overrides as compatibility-sensitive and verify the complete panel after a game update.

Workspace-specific tabs, filters, and merge generators belong to local cards.
